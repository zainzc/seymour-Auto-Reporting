How Restricted Terms Work

Never Introduce terms are prohibited, Requires Authorization terms need a separate approved Seymour Auto rule, Remove as Noise terms are unwanted source text, and Protected terms must be preserved when verified and relevant.

Protected does not mean automatically add the term. It means do not remove a verified relevant term merely to shorten the title. Long Block and Short Block are locked safety restrictions. Runtime title enforcement is not implemented here.
