How Synonym Enrichment Works

Synonyms are optional buyer-search terms, not mandatory replacements. The future optimizer may use an enabled synonym only when it is accurate, useful, not already represented, verified fitment is preserved, and the final title remains within 80 characters.

Terminology Rules normalize wording. Synonyms optionally add search wording.

CV Axle may only be used for a confirmed FRONT half-shaft; never for rear axle or axle housing.
